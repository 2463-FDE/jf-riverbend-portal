## 1. Emergency Warning Signs

Call emergency services for severe symptoms such as trouble breathing, chest pain, sudden weakness on one side, seizure, loss of consciousness, or a severe allergic reaction.

## 2. Glucose-Related Warning Signs

Low blood glucose warning signs can include shaking, sweating, confusion, and irritability. People using insulin or certain other glucose-lowering medicines should follow the plan provided by their care team. This guide does not prescribe a glucose threshold or medication dose.

High blood glucose warning signs can include extreme thirst, frequent urination, nausea, and confusion. Persistent vomiting, inability to keep fluids down, or rapidly worsening confusion is a reason to seek urgent evaluation.

## 3. Automated-System Boundary

Non-emergency questions about a portal result belong with the care team. Retrieval must not invent a diagnosis, perform autonomous triage, or tell a patient to stop a medicine. If urgency cannot be determined safely, the system must use the approved escalation message rather than improvise.
