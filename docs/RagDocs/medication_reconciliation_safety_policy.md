## 1. Purpose

This synthetic policy separates medication education from completion of medication reconciliation.

## 2. Permitted Education

Portal education may explain why an accurate medication list matters. It may repeat approved consumer-safety guidance such as keeping a current list and asking a clinician or pharmacist about possible duplicates.

## 3. Prohibited Conclusions and Actions

Displaying or discussing a medication list does not mean reconciliation is complete. An automated response must not:

- Mark reconciliation complete.
- Add, remove, or change a medication or dose.
- Tell a patient to start or stop a medication.
- Resolve a possible duplicate without clinician or pharmacist review.
- Treat a patient-provided list as a verified prescribing record.

## 4. Escalation

Uncertain, conflicting, or incomplete medication information must be routed to the care team. The agent may explain the limitation but may not resolve it.

## 5. Training Limitation

**This is a fictional policy for a synthetic software demonstration. It is not medication advice and must not be used for real medication reconciliation or treatment decisions.**
